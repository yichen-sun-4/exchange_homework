The confusing pair

PROCESSED is free. PRODUCTION is not.

  • Data can be fresh in PROCESSED while appearing nowhere in git.
  • A view is missing because its file is missing, not because the data is.
  • A new view on a quiet collection may not be applied until you rerun it yourself.

The one decision

Grain is set once, in silver

  • Bronze keeps one row per _id; gold passes the grain through.
  • A row-duplicating join in silver can't be seen in a SUM on a gold dashboard.
  • Prove the grain in DEV, because prod never runs the tests.

Gold is a promise

Name, columns, grain, grant

A reporting model holds no logic. It promises a reader you don't control four things. Each one can break without any error, so the reader just gets a smaller number.
